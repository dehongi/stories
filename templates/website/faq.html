Getting Started

Click the "Sign Up" button in the top navigation bar. Fill in your details, including your name, email, and password. Verify your email address through the confirmation link we'll send you, and you're ready to start!

Writing & Publishing

Click on "Write a Story" from your dashboard, compose your story using our editor, add tags and a category, then click "Publish" when you're ready to share it with the world.

Yes, you can edit your published stories at any time. Just go to your dashboard, find the story you want to edit, and click the "Edit" button.

We encourage creative expression while maintaining a safe and inclusive environment. Please avoid explicit content, hate speech, plagiarism, and ensure proper attribution for any referenced work.

Account & Settings

Go to your profile settings, click on "Security," and follow the prompts to change your password. Make sure to use a strong, unique password.
